Our Process

How We Deliver
Consistent Results

We follow a structured, proven 90-day framework to onboard your business, build momentum, and create compounding growth over time.

Days 1 – 30
Foundation & Discovery

We spend the first month learning your business deeply, auditing everything, and building the right infrastructure before we spend a single dollar or publish a single post.

Week 1

Deep Business Audit

We review your current website, social profiles, Google presence, reviews, and competitors. No guessing — we start with facts.

Deliverable: Full Audit Report
Week 2

Strategy Development

We build a custom 90-day marketing plan tailored to your specific business, industry, location, and growth goals.

Deliverable: 90-Day Marketing Roadmap
Weeks 3–4

Setup & Infrastructure

Accounts configured, tracking installed, Google profile optimized, ad accounts created, and content templates designed.

Deliverable: All Channels Live & Tracking
Days 31 – 60
Launch & Build Momentum

Everything goes live. Ads run, content is published, and we monitor daily to learn what's working and quickly cut what isn't.

Week 5–6

Content & Ads Launch

Social content calendar kicks off, paid ad campaigns go live, and email/SMS sequences are activated and tested.

Deliverable: First Campaigns Active
Week 6–7

Data Collection & Testing

We A/B test ad creatives, post formats, and messaging to learn what resonates most with your specific audience.

Deliverable: Performance Insights Report
Week 7–8

First Results Check-In

We meet to review early results, adjust strategy based on data, and confirm priorities for the final phase of the 90 days.

Deliverable: 30-Day Performance Review
Days 61 – 90
Optimize & Scale

With data in hand, we double down on what's working, cut what isn't, and begin scaling the channels delivering the strongest return.

Week 9–10

Campaign Optimization

Ad budgets shifted to top performers, content strategy refined based on engagement data, SEO adjustments made based on ranking reports.

Deliverable: Optimized Campaign Set
Week 11

Scale What's Working

Increase investment in the highest-ROI channels and expand audience targeting to reach more potential customers in your area.

Deliverable: Expanded Campaign Reach
Week 12

Full 90-Day Report

A complete report covering every metric — leads generated, cost per lead, reach, engagement, ranking improvements, and ROI summary.

Deliverable: Complete 90-Day Growth Report
Month 4 and Beyond
Compounding Long-Term Growth

The first 90 days build the foundation. Everything after compounds on top of it — your presence grows stronger, your cost-per-lead decreases, and results multiply month over month.
